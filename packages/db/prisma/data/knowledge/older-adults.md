- Older adults benefit from the same aerobic and strength guidance as younger adults, plus balance and strength activities on 3 or more days a week to prevent falls (WHO 2020).
- Strength training is safe and effective at any age when progressed gradually; it protects muscle, bone, and independence.
- Protein of about 1.0–1.2 g/kg/day is recommended for healthy older adults, and more for active people or those recovering from illness, unless kidney function is reduced (PROT-AGE 2013).
- Anyone with heart, joint or bone conditions should get medical clearance and start supervised if possible.
