- Total daily calories and protein matter far more than timing for body composition (ISSN nutrient timing 2017).
- Intermittent fasting gives similar weight loss to ordinary daily calorie restriction when calories are matched (Cioffi 2018). It is a valid choice for people who find it easier, not a superior method.
- The post-workout "anabolic window" is wider than once thought: a protein-rich meal within a few hours before or after training is enough.
- A meal with carbohydrate and some protein 1–3 hours before training helps performance; train fasted only if it feels fine.
- People with diabetes, a history of eating disorders, pregnancy, or on medication should not fast without medical advice.
