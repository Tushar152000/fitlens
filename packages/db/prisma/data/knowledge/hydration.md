- Adequate total water intake (drinks plus about 20% from food) is about 2.0 L a day for women and 2.5 L for men in temperate conditions (EFSA). Needs rise with heat, humidity, sweat and exercise, which matters in Indian summers.
- Pale yellow urine is a simple sign of good hydration; dark urine suggests drinking more.
- During exercise, aim to avoid losing more than about 2% of body weight from sweat, and don't drink so much that weight goes up: overdrinking can cause low blood sodium (ACSM).
- For long or very sweaty sessions, fluids with sodium (ORS, nimbu pani with salt, sports drinks) help.
