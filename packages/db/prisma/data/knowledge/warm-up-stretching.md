- Warm up for 5–10 minutes with light cardio and dynamic movements, then do a few lighter sets of the first exercise.
- Long static stretches (over about 60 seconds per muscle) right before strength or power work can slightly reduce performance; short holds within a full warm-up have little effect (Behm 2016).
- To improve flexibility, stretch major muscle groups at least 2–3 days a week, holding each stretch about 10–30 seconds and repeating to reach about 60 seconds per muscle (ACSM 2011).
- Strength training through a full range of motion also improves flexibility.
