- For an uncomplicated pregnancy, with the doctor's approval, about 150 minutes a week of moderate activity is recommended (ACOG 2020). Walking, stationary cycling, swimming, and modified strength training are common choices.
- Avoid contact sports, activities with a high risk of falls, hot yoga or overheating, and lying flat on the back for long periods in later pregnancy.
- Stop and contact a doctor for vaginal bleeding, fluid leakage, regular painful contractions, dizziness, chest pain, headache, calf pain or swelling, or breathlessness before exertion.
- Pregnancy is not a time for weight loss or calorie deficits; FITR8 does not set a deficit when the medical flag is on.
- After birth, return gradually as cleared by the doctor, starting with walking and pelvic floor exercises.
