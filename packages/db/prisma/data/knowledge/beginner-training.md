- Beginners progress well on 2–3 full-body sessions a week, 1–3 sets of about 8–12 reps per exercise (ACSM 2009). WHO advises muscle-strengthening activity on at least 2 days a week for all adults.
- Cover the main movements: a squat or leg press, a hip hinge (Romanian deadlift, hip thrust), a push (push-up, bench or overhead press), a pull (row, lat pulldown), and a core exercise.
- Start light enough to learn the movement, then add weight gradually. Leave 2–3 reps in reserve while learning.
- Some soreness in the first weeks is normal and fades as the body adapts.
- Strength training will not make women "bulky"; it builds strength, bone health and shape.
