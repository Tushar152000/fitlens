- Fat loss needs a sustained energy deficit: eating less energy than the body uses. Diet type (low carb, low fat, fasting) matters far less than adherence when calories and protein are equal (ISSN diets and body composition).
- A rate of about 0.5–1% of body weight a week keeps more muscle and is easier to sustain; faster loss costs more lean mass, especially in lean people (Helms 2014).
- "7,700 kcal = 1 kg of fat" is only a rough short-term rule. Real loss slows over time as the body adapts and weight drops (Hall 2011), so expect progress to slow and judge by trends.
- Keep protein high (see protein note), keep resistance training, and sleep enough: these protect muscle during a deficit.
- Very low intakes (under about 1,200 kcal a day) need medical supervision.
- The user's target already includes their deficit; explain it rather than suggesting a different number.
