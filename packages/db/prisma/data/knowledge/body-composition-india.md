- Asian Indians develop diabetes and heart risk at lower body weights than many other groups. Indian consensus cut-offs are: normal BMI 18.0–22.9, overweight 23.0–24.9, obesity 25 or more (Misra 2009).
- Abdominal obesity for Asian Indians: waist 90 cm or more in men, 80 cm or more in women (Misra 2009). Waist is a better guide to health risk than weight alone.
- BMI can't tell muscle from fat: muscular people can have a high BMI. Waist, photos, strength and how clothes fit add context.
- Body-fat estimates from scales and formulas have large errors; use them for trends, not exact numbers.
