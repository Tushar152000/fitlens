- Adults (18–64): 150–300 minutes a week of moderate aerobic activity (brisk walking, cycling) or 75–150 minutes of vigorous activity (running), or a mix, plus muscle-strengthening on 2 or more days a week (WHO 2020).
- More is better for health beyond these amounts, and some is better than none: even short bouts count.
- Older adults (65+): the same, plus balance and strength activities on 3 or more days a week to prevent falls.
- Children and teens (5–17): an average of 60 minutes a day of moderate-to-vigorous activity, with vigorous activity and muscle and bone strengthening on 3 days a week.
- Limit sitting; replace it with any movement. Breaking up long sitting with short walks helps.
