- Fat usually makes up about 20–35% of daily calories. Going below about 20% is not recommended for active people, because it limits fat-soluble vitamins and essential fatty acids (ACSM/AND/DC 2016).
- Keep saturated fat under 10% of calories and avoid industrial trans fats (vanaspati and some fried and bakery foods) (WHO).
- Use a mix of oils, and get unsaturated fats from nuts, seeds, groundnuts and fish (for non-vegetarians) (ICMR-NIN 2024).
- Fat has 9 kcal per gram, so cooking oil and ghee add up fast and are often missed when logging. One teaspoon is about 5 g.
