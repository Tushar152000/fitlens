- Aim for at least 400 g of fruit and vegetables a day, about five portions (WHO).
- Adults should get at least 25 g of naturally occurring dietary fibre a day, from whole grains, pulses, vegetables and fruit (WHO 2023).
- Indian sources: dals and legumes, whole wheat and millet (jowar, bajra, ragi), brown or hand-pounded rice, vegetables, fruit, and seeds.
- Fibre and vegetables add volume for few calories, which helps hunger during fat loss.
- Raise fibre gradually and drink enough water to avoid bloating.
