- Successful endurance athletes do most of their training, about 80% of sessions, at low intensity, with about 20% at moderate-to-high intensity (Seiler 2010).
- Easy means conversational: you can talk in full sentences (the talk test). For many new runners this pace is slower than expected, and walking breaks are fine.
- Hard sessions (intervals, tempo runs) are added once a base of regular easy running is in place, usually 1–2 a week at most.
- Heart-rate zones from formulas like 220 minus age are rough estimates; the talk test or perceived effort works well for most people.
