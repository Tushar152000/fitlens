- Body weight can swing 0.5–2 kg from day to day because of water, salt, carbohydrate stores (each gram of glycogen is stored with water), food still in the gut, and, for many women, the menstrual cycle. One day's number says little about fat.
- Compare weekly averages, weighed under the same conditions (morning, after the toilet, before eating).
- A real plateau is no change in the weekly average for 2–3 weeks. Before changing anything, check logging accuracy (oil, drinks, snacks, weekends and portion sizes are commonly missed), sleep, and daily steps, which often drop during a diet.
- Starting or increasing training often adds a little water weight at first while fat loss continues; waist measurement and photos help here.
- Weight loss naturally slows as body weight falls, because a lighter body uses less energy (Hall 2011).
