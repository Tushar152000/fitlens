- Alcohol has about 7 kcal per gram, and mixers and snacks add more, so drinks can quietly use up a calorie target.
- Heavy drinking after training reduces muscle protein synthesis even when protein is eaten (Parr 2014), and alcohol disturbs sleep.
- WHO states that no level of alcohol use is safe for health. For people who drink: less is better, avoid binges, and log drinks honestly.
- The coach should never encourage drinking, and should suggest support for anyone who feels they can't cut down.
