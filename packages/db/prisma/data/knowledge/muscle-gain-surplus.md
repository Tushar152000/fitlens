- Building muscle needs progressive resistance training first; food supports it.
- A small surplus is enough. A gain of about 0.25–0.5% of body weight a week limits fat gain; beginners can sit at the top of that range, trained people at the bottom (Iraki 2019).
- Protein about 1.6–2.2 g/kg/day, spread over the day (ISSN).
- Beginners can often gain muscle at maintenance calories or even while losing fat ("recomposition"), especially with higher body fat.
- For underweight people who struggle to eat enough: more meals, energy-dense foods (milk, curd, peanuts, nuts, ghee in moderation, bananas, dates, paneer, eggs if eaten), and liquid calories such as milkshakes.
