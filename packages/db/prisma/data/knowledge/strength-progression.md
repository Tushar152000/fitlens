- Strength improves most with heavier loads (roughly 80% or more of one-rep max, about 1–6 reps) on the lifts you want to get better at, alongside lighter work (ACSM 2009; Schoenfeld 2017).
- Progressive overload: add a little weight or reps over time. Double progression is simple: pick a rep range (say 8–12); when every set reaches the top with good form, add the smallest weight step and start again at the bottom.
- Rest about 2–3 minutes or more between sets of big compound lifts (squat, deadlift, bench, rows); 1–2 minutes is fine for small isolation exercises (ACSM 2009; Schoenfeld 2016).
- Stalls are normal. Check sleep, food and recovery first, then adjust: a lighter week, a small change in reps, or a variation of the lift.
- Technique comes before load. Stop a set when form breaks down.
