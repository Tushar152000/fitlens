- More daily steps are linked with lower risk of early death, with the benefit levelling off around 6,000–8,000 steps a day for adults 60 and over, and around 8,000–10,000 for younger adults (Paluch 2022).
- 10,000 steps is a useful round goal, not a magic threshold; going from very few steps to a few thousand more brings the biggest gain.
- Walking burns meaningful energy over a week and is easy to recover from, which makes it a good tool during fat loss. Daily steps often drop when dieting without people noticing.
- Increase gradually, for example by about 1,000 steps a day each week.
