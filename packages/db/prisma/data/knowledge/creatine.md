- Creatine monohydrate is the most studied and most effective supplement for strength, power and lean mass gains alongside resistance training (ISSN 2017).
- Dose: 3–5 g a day. Optional loading: about 0.3 g/kg/day (roughly 20 g in 4 doses) for 5–7 days fills stores faster; without loading, stores fill in about 3–4 weeks.
- Timing doesn't matter much; consistency does. Take it with water or a meal.
- Expect about 1–2 kg of weight gain early, mostly water inside the muscle; it is not fat.
- In healthy people it has not been shown to harm the kidneys or liver at recommended doses. People with kidney disease, and anyone under 18, should ask their doctor first.
- Vegetarians, who have lower muscle creatine from the diet, may respond more.
- The claim that creatine causes hair loss rests on a single small study and has not been confirmed.
