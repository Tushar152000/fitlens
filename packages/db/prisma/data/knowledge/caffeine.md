- Caffeine improves endurance and many kinds of strength and power performance. Effective doses are about 3–6 mg/kg about 60 minutes before exercise; lower doses (around 2 mg/kg) can also help (ISSN 2021).
- Healthy adults can have up to about 400 mg a day; pregnant women should stay at or below 200 mg a day (EFSA 2015). A cup of filtered coffee has roughly 80–120 mg, a cup of tea or chai much less; values vary.
- Caffeine even 6 hours before bed can disturb sleep (Drake 2013), so avoid it in the afternoon and evening if sleep suffers.
- Pre-workout products often contain high doses and other stimulants; check the label. People with heart conditions, high blood pressure or anxiety should be cautious and ask their doctor.
