- Good vegetarian sources: dals and other pulses (moong, masoor, chana, rajma, lobia), soya chunks, tofu, paneer, curd and hung curd, milk, buttermilk, peanuts, seeds, and sprouts. Eggetarians can add eggs; vegans use soya, tofu, pulses, nuts and seeds.
- Eating cereals and pulses together (dal-rice, roti-dal, idli-sambar) gives a better mix of amino acids than either alone (ICMR-NIN).
- Plant proteins are slightly less digestible and lower in leucine, so somewhat larger servings or a mix of sources work best. Soya is the most complete plant protein.
- Protein powders (whey, or soy or pea for vegans) are a convenience, not a necessity.
