- Volume: more weekly sets per muscle give more growth up to a point. 10 or more hard sets per muscle per week beat fewer than 5 (Schoenfeld 2017). Around 10–20 hard sets per muscle a week is a common evidence-based range; beginners grow well at the low end.
- Reps: anywhere from about 6 to 30 reps builds similar muscle if sets are taken close to failure; heavier loads build more maximal strength (Schoenfeld 2017).
- Effort: most working sets should end about 1–3 reps short of failure. Going to failure on every set adds fatigue without clear extra benefit, especially on big lifts.
- Frequency: training each muscle at least twice a week is a practical way to spread the volume (Schoenfeld 2016).
- Progressive overload (more weight, reps or sets over time) drives continued gains.
