- Carbohydrates fuel hard and long exercise. Daily needs rise with training load (ACSM/AND/DC 2016):
  - light, low-intensity or skill training: 3–5 g/kg/day
  - moderate exercise about 1 hour a day: 5–7 g/kg/day
  - 1–3 hours a day of moderate-to-high intensity endurance training: 6–10 g/kg/day
  - over 4–5 hours a day: 8–12 g/kg/day
- For general fitness and fat loss, carbs are not fattening by themselves; total calories decide fat change. Low-carb diets work when they help someone eat less, not because of a special effect (ISSN).
- Eating carbs at night does not cause fat gain by itself.
- Prefer whole grains, millets, pulses, fruit and vegetables for fibre; keep added sugar low.
