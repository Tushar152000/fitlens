- Sessions under about an hour need no fuel during the run.
- Longer sessions: about 30–60 g of carbohydrate an hour; for events over about 2.5 hours, up to about 90 g an hour using mixed glucose and fructose sources (Jeukendrup 2014; ACSM/AND/DC 2016). Practise fuelling in training.
- For races over about 90 minutes, carbohydrate loading of about 10–12 g/kg/day for 36–48 hours beforehand tops up glycogen (ACSM/AND/DC 2016).
- Drink to limit sweat losses to about 2% of body weight, and use sodium in long, hot events; don't overdrink (ACSM 2007).
- Simple Indian options: bananas, dates, chikki, rice-based meals before, and ORS or salted nimbu pani during.
