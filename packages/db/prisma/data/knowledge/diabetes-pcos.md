- General guidance only; medication and targets are for the user's doctor.
- Diabetes and prediabetes: at least 150 minutes a week of moderate-to-vigorous aerobic activity, resistance training 2–3 days a week, and breaking up long sitting about every 30 minutes all improve blood sugar control (ADA). People on insulin or sulphonylureas should know how to prevent and treat low blood sugar around exercise.
- PCOS: healthy lifestyle (diet and exercise) is first-line for everyone with PCOS; in those with excess weight, losing 5–10% of body weight can improve symptoms (2023 international guideline). No single "PCOS diet" is proven superior.
- Steady carbohydrate portions with protein and fibre help blood sugar; whole grains, millets, pulses and vegetables are good choices.
- Any change to medicines, fasting, or very low-carb diets must be discussed with the doctor.
