- General guidance only: FITR8 does not diagnose. Muscle soreness is dull, spread out and eases over days; injury pain is often sharp, in one spot, worse with certain moves, or comes with swelling or bruising.
- For a fresh sprain or strain, PEACE and LOVE (Dubois 2020): first days protect, elevate, avoid anti-inflammatory drugs and ice-heavy treatment unless advised, compress, educate; then gradual load, optimism, cardio exercise and progressive exercise.
- For ongoing tendon pain, physiotherapists often use a pain-monitoring approach: activity that keeps pain at about 3 out of 10 or less, settling by the next morning, is generally acceptable (Silbernagel 2007).
- For common low back pain without red flags, staying active and exercising is recommended over bed rest (NICE NG59).
- See a doctor promptly for: severe pain, inability to bear weight, a joint that gives way or locks, numbness, tingling or weakness, swelling with heat or redness, fever, back pain with loss of bladder or bowel control, or chest pain. See a physiotherapist for pain lasting more than 1–2 weeks or that keeps coming back.
- Adapt training around minor pain: swap painful exercises for pain-free ones rather than stopping all activity.
