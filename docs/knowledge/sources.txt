FITR8 KNOWLEDGE SOURCES
Reference books and guidelines for the AI coach: exercise science, strength training, running,
physiotherapy, nutrition and sports nutrition. Use the latest edition of each book.
Updated: 2026-11-18

NOTES
- Almost all books here are copyrighted. Their text must NOT be loaded into the AI coach (RAG,
  embeddings, prompts) without a licence from the publisher. Use them to write and check the coach's
  rules and answers. The coach itself should quote the open guidelines and position stands in
  section 12.
- Books fall a few years behind current research. For anything new, check the position stands,
  systematic reviews and meta-analyses (PubMed, Cochrane).
- No set of sources makes the AI always right. Accuracy also needs answers that cite their source,
  plus the coach sending users to a doctor or physio for medical or injury questions.

    - caffeine
